# WAIT

Your brain won't shut up. The urge to check, wash, fix, or just *do something* is loud as hell. But you're not doing it. You're here instead. You're gonna sit with it for a few minutes.

This is WAIT. A super simple web tool for when OCD has the wheel and you just need to hit pause.

It's not therapy. It's not a cure. It's just a timer, a reminder that you can sit with the uncomfortable stuff, and some proof on a screen that your anxiety actually goes down when you don't give in.

## What it actually does

**The timer (the main thing)**

Rate how anxious you feel right now (1–10). Pick what compulsion you're fighting — checking, washing, needing to fix something, or that thought that just won't leave. Choose how long you're willing to wait. Write down the fear your brain is selling you. Hit start.

Timer runs. Anxiety slider is right there. Distraction prompts if you need them. Breathing circle too. The app doesn't try to entertain you. It just gets out of the way so you can sit with the discomfort.

When time's up: Did you hold off? Log it. Did you give in anyway? Log that too. No judgment either way. You get the data — how long you lasted, how your anxiety shifted, and some pattern tracking over time.

**Breathe**

Simple in-hold-out guide. One click, the circle animates. Useful when your nervous system is freaking out.

**Distraction prompts**

Random little thoughts to yank your brain somewhere else. Sometimes dumb ("favorite pasta shape"). Sometimes actually useful ("what would you tell a friend who had this thought"). Hit "another one" as many times as you need.

**Note to yourself**

Write something you need to hear when your brain is lying. "It always passes." "I've done this before." "This feeling is temporary." Keep it visible. Change it whenever.

**Recent attempts**

"Lately" shows what you've been fighting recently. Patterns start showing up. You can see that you keep trying, and that anxiety does drop over time.

**The pile**

That stuck thought that won't leave? Dump it in the pile. Sometimes just acknowledging it helps your brain let go a little. You can save the pile as a file or wipe it clean. Your call.

**Privacy**

Optional PIN lock. Everything stays on your device. No servers. Nobody's reading your thoughts. Ever.

**Works offline, works everywhere**

Phone, tablet, desktop — doesn't matter. No install. Just open it. Works without internet.
